Frequently asked questions

Does Sloan replace my VA?

It replaces immediate first-touch calling and fixed qualification. It does not replace deeper discovery, CRM work, underwriting, negotiation, or closing.

Can we change the script?

No. During the current pilot, every client uses the same qualification flow we use in our own operation.

Will you connect Sloan to our CRM?

We provide a standard dashboard or webhook payload. Your team is responsible for connecting it to your CRM.

What happens when a seller does not answer?

Sloan makes up to 10 attempts. If no conversation occurs, the lead is marked not reached.

What happens when a call is incomplete?

We provide the available transcript and mark the call incomplete so your team can follow up.

Can we get the call recording?

Yes, where recording is legally permitted. Recordings are available on request.

Do you offer cold calling?

No. Sloan calls only inbound leads that the client represents have provided legally sufficient consent.

Why we built Sloan

We did not start by trying to build an AI product.

We were already generating inbound seller leads inside Decimal. We wanted every new lead called immediately, followed up consistently, and returned to our acquisitions team with the same qualification questions answered.

A human team can do many things Sloan cannot. But one person cannot be available every second of every day, make several calls at once, and follow the same cadence for every lead.

So we built Sloan to handle that narrow first-touch job.

We still use it in our own operation. Now we are opening the same fixed system to a small number of other real estate operators.
