Honest limitations

Sloan is very good at one job. It is not perfect.

We would rather be clear about where Sloan stops than promise it replaces work it does not perform.

Unusual conversations

Some sellers answer in ways that fall outside the supported qualification flow.

Interruptions and dead air

Transcript errors

Addresses, prices and other important details should be verified before being used in an offer or contract.

No human rescue

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Sloan replace my VA?

No. Sloan takes first-touch dialing and qualification. Your VA works the sellers who already answered and qualified.

Can we change the script?

You can read every question before you pay, but the flow is fixed. That standardization is what keeps setup to one day and the price flat.

Will you connect Sloan to our CRM?

We deliver to a shared dashboard or a webhook. We do not build automations inside your CRM, and you do not have to change it.

What happens when a seller does not answer?

Sloan continues through the follow-up sequence, up to 10 attempts, then marks the lead not reached with every attempt logged.

What happens when a call is incomplete?

We flag it, send whatever was captured, and your team finishes the conversation. We do not hide those calls.

Can we get the call recording?

Transcripts come standard with every call. Recordings are available on request.

Do you offer cold calling?

No. Sloan calls inbound leads who asked to be contacted. No purchased lists and no skip-traced numbers.

How long until it is live?

One working day after we confirm fit, including test calls on an answered and an unanswered lead.

Why we built Sloan

We did not start by trying to build an AI product. We were already generating inbound seller leads of our own, and we wanted every new lead called immediately, followed up consistently, and returned to our acquisitions team with the same qualification questions answered.

A human team can do many things Sloan cannot. But one person cannot be available every second of every day, make several calls at once, and follow the same cadence for every lead. So we built Sloan to handle that narrow first-touch job.

We still use it in our own operation. Now we are opening the same fixed system to a small number of other real estate operators.
