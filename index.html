What you're probably thinking

"Sellers will hate talking to a robot."

Some do. Far more hate leaving a voicemail on Saturday and hearing back Tuesday. Listen to the recording and judge it against the alternative, which is silence.

"Do I have to change my CRM?"

No. We never touch it. Leads come to our webhook, results go to your dashboard or endpoint, your CRM stays exactly as it is.

"Should I fire my VA?"

We'd tell you not to. Sloan takes first-touch dialing; your VA gets the sellers who already answered and qualified.

"I could build this myself for less."

You could. It took us months of real seller calls to make the script hold up. If you want to own and maintain that, a self-service tool is genuinely the better buy.

"What if it doesn't work for us?"

The pilot is 30 days for exactly that reason. At the end you'll have your own connection numbers and can walk away — month to month, no notice period.

"Is this legal?"

We only call inbound leads who opted in, the agent discloses what it is, and consent language on your forms is part of onboarding. No cold lists, no exceptions.
