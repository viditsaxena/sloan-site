What you are probably thinking

The four objections we hear on almost every call, answered the way we answer them there.

"Sellers will hate talking to a robot."

On 95% of calls, they cannot even tell it is a robot. A few will guess, and a few genuinely will not like it. But far more sellers hate leaving a voicemail on Saturday and hearing back on Tuesday. Listen to the call above and judge for yourself, then compare it to the real alternative, which is silence.

"I could just hire another VA."

You could, and many of our clients keep theirs. But look at where a VA's day actually goes: most dials never connect at all, so most of a shift is spent dialing numbers that ring out, not talking to sellers. We measured this on ourselves. One more VA still cannot work at 9pm, answer a call-back while already on a call, or make ten attempts on every lead. Sloan absorbs that wasted-dialing layer, so your VA is only ever talking to sellers who answered.

"We already have an AI tool we never set up."

That is the usual story. Sloan is a managed service, not software you configure. You send leads, we run the calling, you get answers. There is nothing to build and nothing to maintain.

"What if it does not work for us?"

It is month to month from day one, no long contract to sign. If it is not working, you cancel and walk away with your own connect and qualification numbers already set up.

Why we built Sloan

We run a wholesaling operation. We buy inbound seller leads, and we pay people to call them.

One month we actually looked at what happened to every lead we paid for. A lead would come in at nine at night and get its first call the next morning. A seller would ring back and hit voicemail because the person was on another call. A lead would get two or three attempts and then quietly stop being worked. None of it was anyone's fault. One person cannot answer at 9pm, be on two calls at once, and make a tenth attempt on a lead they last thought about a week ago.

So we built the calling desk we wanted, tested the script on our own distressed sellers, and ran it until it was boring. Now every lead gets called in seconds and our team spends its day talking to people who actually picked up.

Sloan is that exact system. We are opening it to a small number of other operators — same script, same cadence, same output.
